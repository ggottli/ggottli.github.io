Settings

Card engine — what decides a card's shape

A tiny model that lives in this page (about 110 KB, trained on example phrases) votes together with hand-written rules. It only picks the shape; dates, math, units and money are always computed by code. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Your card shapes

Untick any you don’t want — they’ll never be auto-detected or suggested. Existing cards keep their shape.

Canvas

Look

Backgrounds, colors, fonts and light/dark live in the Style panel in the toolbar.

Sync across devices (GitHub Gist)

Create a token with only the gist scope at github.com/settings/tokens. It stays in this browser and is never written into backups.

Data

Everything lives in this browser: text in local storage, images in IndexedDB. Back up often.
