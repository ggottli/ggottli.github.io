Sprawla canvas for messy thinking

Click anywhere and type. A thought becomes a note. 20 min stretch becomes a timer. coffee with maya thursday 10am becomes an event with a calendar button. You arrange the rest.

  1. Type. Click the canvas, write, press Enter.
  2. It shapes itself. 22 kinds of card, picked instantly on your device. Press Tab or type / to choose yourself.
  3. You arrange. Drag, connect with the ● handle, group in boxes, drop one card onto another to combine or snap into a list.

Everything stays in this browser. No account, nothing is sent anywhere.
