What the coach looks at

Move ratings compare the engine's evaluation before and after your move. "Best" means you found the engine's top choice. A blunder is a drop of more than 2.5 pawns.

A piece is hanging when it is attacked and either undefended or attacked by something cheaper. Pins are drawn as lines from the pinning piece through the pinned piece to the piece behind it. A fork is one piece attacking two or more targets that are undefended or worth more than it is.
