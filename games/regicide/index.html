Regicide (solo)

How to play

Goal. Defeat all 12 royals in order: four Jacks (10 attack, 20 health), then four Queens (15 / 30), then four Kings (20 / 40). You lose if you can't cover an enemy's attack with discards, or if you begin a turn with no cards and no Jesters.

Each turn.

  1. Play. One card, an Ace with one other card (any card, even another Ace or a royal), or a set of same-number cards from 2 to 5 whose total is 10 or less (2-2, 2-2-2, 2-2-2-2, 3-3, 3-3-3, 4-4, 5-5). Aces can't join sets. Solo has no yielding: if you hold cards, you must play.
  2. Suit powers fire at the total value played. ♥ heal: that many cards from the discard go, shuffled, under the Tavern deck. ♦ draw: draw that many (hand max 8). ♣ double: damage counts twice. ♠ shield: the enemy's attack drops by that much, cumulative until it's defeated. Mixed suits all fire at the full total; a matching pair of suits fires once. ♥ resolves before ♦. The enemy ignores the power of its own suit (the number still counts as damage).
  3. Damage. If total damage reaches the enemy's health it dies: played cards go to the discard, and you skip its attack and start a fresh turn against the next royal. Exact damage puts the royal face down on top of the Tavern deck instead of the discard; draw it and it fights for you (Jack = 10, Queen = 15, King = 20, normal suit power).
  4. Suffer. Otherwise the enemy hits for its attack minus shields. Discard cards worth at least that much (Ace = 1). An empty hand is allowed.

Jesters. You have two. At the start of step 1 (before playing) or step 4 (before discarding), flip one: discard your whole hand and draw 8. Win with 0 / 1 / 2 flipped for a Gold / Silver / Bronze victory.

Card values. 2–10 face value, Ace 1, Jack 10, Queen 15, King 20 — the same whether attacking or discarding.
